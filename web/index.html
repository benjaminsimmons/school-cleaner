School Cleaner

Hides a school's details in Word documents before an AI such as Claude sees them, then puts them back into the finished work.

Runs only in this browser. Documents are never uploaded; works offline.

School details to hide

One per line: the school's name written every way it appears, short forms, the address, staff names. At least 3 characters each. These stay in this browser.

Add the Word documents (.docx) you want Claude to work on.

Pictures

Click a picture to switch it between Hide and Keep. A hidden picture becomes a white box of the same size, and comes back at the end. Every picture starts as Hide.

      Keep a backup

      The hidden details and pictures are kept in this browser only. If the browser's data is cleared, finished work can no longer be put back, so save a backup after cleaning. The backup holds the real details: keep it away from Claude.
